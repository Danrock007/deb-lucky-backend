# Product image sources

The product photos that already existed in
`client/public/products/` are imported into Django's `MEDIA_ROOT/products/`
and served through the product API's absolute image URLs.

The `Gold Fashion Ring` did not have a matching frontend photo. Its initial
sample image is the Wikimedia Commons file [Gold ring MET DP109248](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gold_ring_MET_DP109248.jpg),
released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
It depicts a historic museum artifact and is only a temporary catalog sample;
replace it with the shop's actual product photo when available.
